i Clay minerals from the kaolinite group are possibly associated with the ochres and umbers.

ii Alunite is one of a group of white-yellowish minerals of formula AM3(SO4)2(OH)6, where A is normally K+ or Na+ and M is Al3+. If M is Fe3+, the mineral is jarosite. While jarosite has been found used on a number of portraits (see 1922.4798 and 1922.4799), alunite is less commonly identified (see Stenger et al. and Smith et al. in same volume). In this case, Na-rich alunite was identified with FTIR through the ν3(SO4)2- at c. 1219 and 1091 cm-1, δ(OH) at c. 1028 cm-1, and structural stretchings and water modes in the 3000-3800 cm-1 region (Bishop and Murad 2005).

iii Arsenic is only found in the yellow pendant gem hanging from the necklace (see fig. 13.8).
